# EU AI Act — raw captures

Primary-source captures for **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689** (the "Artificial Intelligence Act").
Append-only; refresh by adding a new dated file. The compiled, cited page is
`docs/regulatory/eu-ai-act.md`.

## Authoritative source

- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024
  laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act).
- Published in the Official Journal **OJ L, 2024/1689, 12 July 2024**; in force.
- ELI (authoritative): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj
- CELEX: `32024R1689` — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689
- EFTA / EEA-Lex factsheet (EEA-relevance + incorporation status):
  https://www.efta.int/eea-lex/32024r1689

The full Official Journal text is large; rather than scrape the whole OJ document, the
load-bearing articles, annex points and recitals are captured **verbatim** in
`2026-06-23-ai-act-key-provisions.md` (each quote attributed to its article URL). Article
numbers are identical across the OJ text and the navigable reproductions below, so citations
in `docs/regulatory/eu-ai-act.md` reference **article/annex/recital numbers** (stable) and the
verbatim capture.
